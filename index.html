About

Background & What I Build

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Sir Syed University of Engineering & Technology (SSUET) in Karachi, currently working full-time as an AI Engineer at Irvinei.

Most of my day-to-day work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning and backend software engineering. At Irvinei, I architected a 15-stage autonomous model training pipeline using LangGraph, FastAPI, and MongoDB, and previously contributed to the computer vision stack for a commercial consumer security device shipped to customers.

I enjoy taking messy, manual technical workflows—like finding datasets, cleaning corrupt annotations, estimating GPU memory, tuning YOLO models, or querying scattered logs—and turning them into reliable, self-recovering software systems with clean APIs.

Right now, I'm focused on multi-agent orchestration with deterministic guardrails, hybrid vector + structured snapshot retrieval (RAG), and low-latency model deployment using ONNX and TensorRT.
